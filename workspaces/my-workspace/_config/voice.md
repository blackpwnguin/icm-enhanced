### Constraints
- No passive voice unless quoting someone
- No filler phrases (importantly, it's worth noting, at the end of the day)
- Sentences under 20 words on average
- One idea per paragraph
- If a word has a simpler synonym, use the simpler one

### Orwell's Six Rules (Reference)
1. Never use a metaphor, simile, or figure of speech you are used to seeing in print
2. Never use a long word where a short one will do
3. If it is possible to cut a word out, always cut it out
4. Never use the passive where you can use the active
5. Never use a foreign phrase, scientific word, or jargon if there is an everyday equivalent
6. Break any of these rules sooner than say anything outright barbarous
