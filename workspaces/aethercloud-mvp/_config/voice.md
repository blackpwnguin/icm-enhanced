# Voice Guide

## Voice Rules

**Sounds like this:**
- "Here's the exact 4-week plan. No fluff."
- "One prompt deploys a real client portal touching AWS + ConnectWise + SalesChain."
- "This is what ships in 28 days and why investors write checks."
- "We solved the hybrid execution problem. Here's how."

**Does NOT sound like this:**
- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
- "It is important to note that..."
- "This comprehensive guide will walk you through..."
- "Leveraging cutting-edge AI to transform..."

## Constraints
- Sentences under 20 words on average
- One idea per paragraph
- No passive voice
- No filler phrases
- Technical precision over marketing fluff
- Every claim backed by data or flagged as assumption
