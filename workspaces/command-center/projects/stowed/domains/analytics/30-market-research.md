## 2026-07-12 — Measurement approach
- B2B SaaS with a low-volume, high-intent audience (100 beta testers cap) → optimise
  for **conversion quality**, not raw traffic. A small number of well-defined events
  (CTA → waitlist/app-store) tells more than pageview totals.
- "Heard about us" already includes **AI assistant** as a channel — worth a UTM/tag
  strategy to attribute AI-referred traffic.
