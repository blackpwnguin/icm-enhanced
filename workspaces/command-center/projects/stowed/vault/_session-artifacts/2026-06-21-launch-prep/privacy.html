This Privacy Policy explains what information Stowed collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Stowed is an inventory and business-to-business order management application for small and mid size businesses. By using Stowed you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Stowed is operated by Nihilo Solutions LLC ("we", "us", "Stowed"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@stowedapp.app. Our mailing address is [Placeholder: company mailing address].

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your name, email address, and the organization you belong to, along with your role (Owner, Manager, or Employee).
  • Business data you enter. The information you put into Stowed to run your business, such as products, inventory, warehouses, your customers, customer-specific pricing, orders, invoices, and payment records. You control this content, and it belongs to your organization.
  • Subscription information. When you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of your subscription status, not your payment card details.
  • Device and usage information. Basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version, along with diagnostic and crash data that helps us find and fix problems.

How we use information

  • To provide the app and its features, including authentication, order management, pricing, and financial tracking.
  • To keep each organization's data separate and secure.
  • To process and confirm subscriptions.
  • To diagnose problems, monitor stability, and improve the app.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important notices.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate Stowed, and only to the extent needed for them to perform their work. These providers include:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Apple, which processes subscription purchases through the App Store.
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscription status.
  • Sentry, which collects diagnostic and crash reports.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our service.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to keep certain records by law.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including isolation of each organization's data, access controls based on role, and encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
