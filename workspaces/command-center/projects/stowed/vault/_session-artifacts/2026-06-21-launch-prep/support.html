Need help? Email us at support@stowedapp.app. We aim to respond within two business days.

Common questions

  • How do roles work? Stowed has three roles: Owner, Manager, and Employee. Owners and Managers handle pricing and customer management. Employees run the order and fulfillment flow.
  • How does someone join my organization? They sign up and enter your warehouse code. The request stays pending until an Owner approves it, so no one gets access automatically.
  • How is my data kept separate from other businesses? Each organization's data is isolated at the database level, so one business can never see another's information.
  • How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription, and turn off auto-renewal, in your Apple Account settings.

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your account and your personal information at any time:

  • Email support@stowedapp.app from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account".
  • We will confirm the request and remove your personal information within a reasonable period, except for records we are required to keep by law.
  • Canceling a subscription is done separately in your Apple Account settings, since billing is handled by Apple.
