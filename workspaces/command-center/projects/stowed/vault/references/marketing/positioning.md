# Positioning

Working positioning, subject to change once the backend ships and real users land.

Stowed is a subscription iOS app for inventory and B2B order management, for small businesses that sell repeat orders to business customers at customer-specific prices. The pitch is the bundle: scan stock in, sell to customers at their negotiated price, watch receivables and profit, without an ERP.

The demo persona during the UI phase is a small coffee roaster (Northbeam Coffee Co.). That is mock data, not a claim about the ICP. Real ICP work is pending.

For who the app is for, see [[product-overview]]. For pricing, see [[pricing-tiers]].
