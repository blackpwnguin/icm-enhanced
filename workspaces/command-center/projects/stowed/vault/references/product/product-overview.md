# Product overview

Stowed is a subscription iOS app for inventory and B2B order management. It started as an inventory tracker. Ish pushed the scope into order management with customer-specific pricing and basic financial tracking, which is the version being built.

## Who it's for

Small businesses that run a warehouse, sell to repeat business customers at customer-specific prices, and want simple visibility into receivables and gross/net profit without an ERP.

The current demo persona is a coffee roaster (Northbeam Coffee Co.), used as mock data during the UI phase. That is dev fixture, not a claim about the ICP.
